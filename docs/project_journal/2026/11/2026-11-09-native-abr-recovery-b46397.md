## Follow-up Design
- Explicit upstream 503 is preserved as sanitized temporary unavailability with a bounded retry hint and route regressions. Upstream bodies, credentials and arbitrary headers are not forwarded.
- This protocol correction is not a demonstrated explanation of the failed upshift. [Apple's HLS error-handling guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2017/514/) distinguishes temporary server unavailability from invalid gateway responses; it does not promise a fixed rendition-recovery deadline.
- Prefix-seeding, SIDX route coverage and bounded passive buffer observations are implemented without replacing the merged cache/header foundations. Discontinuous loaded ranges remain explicit. Keep server-side whole-asset fill unchanged.
- Do not infer a unique cause from the measured forward buffer. A finite client buffer preference would be a deliberate policy experiment, not a hard cap or an accepted fix; no such override is currently applied.
- The operator chose to reuse buffered content and recover quality for future unbuffered media. Opportunistic higher-quality replacement after the current fragment or an approximately 20-second safety window is permitted. This is not a 20-second forward-buffer cap or a requirement to discard safe low-quality content. Keep the automatic buffer preference and server-side whole-asset downloading unchanged.
- Higher-quality future media retains its own representation/cache identity. Replacement is a playback-scheduling decision at a compatible media boundary, never an in-place rewrite of low-rendition bytes or an ETag/Range-integrity exception. Downloading high media alone is not proof that AVPlayer replaced an already loaded low-rendition buffer.
- Completed transcoding now derives canonical bandwidth from known published MP4 ranges through the shared RFC helper, with 16,384-range and 1,000,000-candidate limits. Unavailable measurements retain the existing estimate rather than being labeled measured. Variant, request and nested metadata stay consistent through save/restore; planning ceilings, encoding, quota and cache identity remain unchanged. Parser/cache regressions and strict native publication/decoding passed during development; final-head delivery gates still require revalidation.

## Resolved Scenario Design
- The operator explicitly selected bounded long recovery acceptance, with latency optimization delivered separately. This changes the required observation window, not the decoded recovery witness or playback policy. Historical 130-second recovery failures remain negative evidence and are not relabeled as passes.
- Preserve the short source-unavailable scenario as a buffered-continuity acceptance case: require high-to-low decoding, recovery-period steady playback, proof that the loaded range reaches the asset end, and every existing control. It cannot waive recovery for arbitrary missing future data.
- Use a separate 3,600-second source-unavailable scenario with a 600-second probe, 720-second cooperative deadline and 840-second inner case limit. Require three distinct decoded samples in each high/low/high phase, actual recovered-high origin traffic, and a passive recovery-time snapshot proving future media remains unbuffered. Missing preconditions or recovered-high frames fail the case rather than becoming a skip. The historical diagnostic case stays explicitly excluded and cannot substitute for this required case.
- Use a fresh controller-written recovery marker and a passive probe observation on the same monotonic clock. The buffered case keeps the original 110-second steady-play window even when both size batches finish early. The future case retains all three decoded-size batches before controls; no seek or player reset may produce its recovery samples.
- The long source profile is bounded at 1,280 MiB total (high 960, low 256, audio 64 MiB), with a 2 GiB managed cache and 512 KiB playlist preflight. Preserve the short cases' original source/cache limits. Require at least 6 GiB free for the large fixture work. Keep report, artifact and per-command byte limits; the aggregate job reserves 25 minutes for compilation, 24 for execution and one for finalization, at most 50 minutes total. All three named native cases must pass.
- These scenario distinctions implement the clarified product contract; historical failed short recovery reports remain retained and are not relabeled as passes. The future-content case retains the full automatic recovery requirement.
